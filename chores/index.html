Privacy Policy — Chore Tracker: Cleaning Plan

Last updated: 6 November 2026 (v1.0)

Chore Tracker: Cleaning Plan (package com.bok.chores) keeps a cleaning schedule for the rooms in your home, reminds you once a day about what is due, and keeps a log of what you did. This page explains what the app does with information.

Your data stays on your phone

Your rooms, chores, schedules, the names you assign chores to, notes, points and your cleaning history are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that receives any of it. None of these values are included in the analytics described below.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account, not by us.

Information the app sends

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how it is used and to find crashes. What is sent is a short record of which feature was used:

  • That a room or chore was added, edited or deleted, whether it started from one of the built-in chore types or was typed by hand, the kind of schedule (for example "weekly"), whether someone was assigned, and how many chores you have as a range (for example "10_19").
  • That a chore was marked done, snoozed, skipped or undone, whether it was done one by one or with "Done all", and how many days late it was as a range; that all of today's chores were finished.
  • That a history entry was edited, that vacation mode was turned on or off (with the paused days as a range), and that a 15-minute quick clean was started or finished.
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, whether it was shown or opened, and whether notification permission was granted.
  • That a backup was exported or imported, with the number of chores as a range and whether it succeeded.
  • If something went wrong, a fixed error code and a shortened error message with any file path removed.

The app does not send:

  • Room names, chore names, notes or the names you assign chores to.
  • The dates of your cleaning history.
  • The contents of an exported or imported file.
  • Your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Firebase itself adds some technical information to every record: an app instance identifier, your device model, Android version, app version, session timing, and an approximate region worked out from your IP address. Crashlytics adds a crash stack and device details when the app crashes.

Advertising

The app is free, with no limit on rooms or chores and every feature available without payment. It is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen and, occasionally, a full-screen ad. To serve and measure them, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and an approximate location worked out from your IP address.

A full-screen ad can appear only after you finish all of today's chores and close the celebration screen, and not every time. It never appears when you open or leave the app, or when you check off a single chore. If an ad fails to load or is blocked, reminders, check-offs and history work exactly the same.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states, a consent or privacy form may be shown using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where it applies, you can reopen it at any time from Ad privacy choices in Settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications — for the daily reminder of today's chores, at the time you choose. On Android 13 and later the app asks once after you set up your first rooms, and again if you turn the reminder on later. You can say no; the rest of the app still works.
  • Run at startup — after your phone restarts, Android clears scheduled reminders. The app sets the daily reminder again.

Firebase and the advertising SDK add a small number of background permissions that Android does not prompt you about: internet access, network state, a wake lock, an attribution permission used by analytics, and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob.

Backup: exporting and importing

Export asks Android to let you pick where to save the file, and writes your rooms, chores and history there. Import asks you to pick a file and reads only that file. The app has no other access to your storage, and the file never passes through us. What happens to an exported file afterwards is up to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Beyond the anonymous usage records and the advertising identifier described above, it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any room, chore or history entry in the app, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Device Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. You can ask for the analytics records associated with your installation to be deleted by emailing the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything further that touches your information, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
