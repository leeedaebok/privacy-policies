Privacy Policy — 1 Second a Day: Video Diary

Last updated: 8 November 2026 (v1.0)

1 Second a Day: Video Diary (package com.bok.secondaday) lets you save a short clip for each day, from the camera or from your gallery, and join them into one movie. This page explains what the app does with information.

Your videos stay on your phone

Clips you record or pick are saved only on your device, in the Movies/SecondADay folder, and the movies you make are saved in Movies/SecondADay Movies. The app never uploads your videos, photos, notes or project names. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that receives them. They are not included in the analytics described below.

Because the clips are ordinary files on your phone, they stay there if you uninstall the app and they appear in your gallery. If you turn on a backup service such as Google Photos for that folder, that service handles the backup under its own terms, not us. If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include the app's list of days, notes and project names in your device backup.

Information the app sends

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how it is used and to find crashes. What is sent is a short record of which feature was used:

  • That a clip was saved, whether it came from the camera, a video or a photo, its length (for example "1s"), whether it was for today or an earlier day, and how many clips the project has as a range (for example "10_49").
  • That a clip was deleted, that a project was added, renamed, switched or deleted, and its shape (vertical, wide or square).
  • That a movie was made: the date range type (for example "this month"), the number of clips as a range, the quality (720p or 1080p), whether music was added, whether it succeeded, how long it took as a range, and whether it was then played or shared.
  • That the clip folder was reconnected and how many clips were found as a range.
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, whether it was shown or opened, and whether notification permission was granted.
  • Whether Pro was turned on by watching an ad, and which Pro feature you were trying to use.
  • If something went wrong, a fixed error code and a shortened error message with any file path removed.

The app does not send:

  • Your videos, photos, music files or any frame from them.
  • Notes, project names, file names or the dates of your clips.
  • Your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Firebase itself adds some technical information to every record: an app instance identifier, your device model, Android version, app version, session timing, and an approximate region worked out from your IP address. Crashlytics adds a crash stack and device details when the app crashes.

Advertising

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and, occasionally, a full-screen ad. To serve and measure them, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and an approximate location worked out from your IP address.

A full-screen ad can appear only after you save today's first clip and close the "Today is saved" card, and not every time. It never appears when you open or leave the app, while recording, while a movie is being made or while a video plays.

Pro for a day: extra features (longer clips, a second clip per day, 1080p movies, your own music and no ads) can be turned on for a limited time by choosing to watch a rewarded ad. This is optional and there is no subscription. If the ad cannot be shown, Pro is turned on anyway. Clips and movies you make while Pro is on remain yours after it ends.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states, a consent or privacy form may be shown using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where it applies, you can reopen it at any time from Ad privacy choices in Settings.

Permissions

  • Camera — to record your daily clip inside the app. If you say no, you can still pick clips from your gallery.
  • Microphone — to record the sound of your clip. If you say no, clips are recorded without sound.
  • Storage (Android 9 and earlier only) — to save clips into your Movies folder. Android 10 and later need no storage permission for this.
  • Notifications — for the daily reminder on days you have not saved a clip yet, at the time you choose. On Android 13 and later the app asks after you save your first clip.
  • Run at startup — after your phone restarts, Android clears scheduled reminders. The app sets the daily reminder again.

The app does not ask for access to all your photos and videos. When you pick from your gallery, Android's photo picker gives the app only the item you chose. When you reconnect your clip folder, Android lets you choose the folder, and the app reads only that folder. Music you add is read only from the file you pick.

Firebase and the advertising SDK add a small number of background permissions that Android does not prompt you about: internet access, network state, a wake lock, an attribution permission used by analytics, and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Beyond the anonymous usage records and the advertising identifier described above, it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any clip or project in the app (and choose whether its video files are deleted too), delete clips from your gallery, or remove the app's data by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID under Device Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. You can ask for the analytics records associated with your installation to be deleted by emailing the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything further that touches your information, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
